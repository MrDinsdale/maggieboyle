Maggie Boyle

24th December 1956 ~ 6th November 2014

I am so very very sorry to have to post here that the beautiful, gracious, brave Maggie Boyle passed away 6th November 2014, peacefully held in love by her family on behalf of all her loved ones, far and wide, who could not be there.

We are immeasurably grateful for the astounding love, care, kindness and generosity shown to Maggie over the past years by countless people in her extended family of the folk community and beyond. This was a mighty gift to her that I know moved her heart deeply. Thank you.

This website will be kept in respect of Maggie's music and life, although it can never pay tribute in full to the wealth and warmth she bestowed on all who met her.
